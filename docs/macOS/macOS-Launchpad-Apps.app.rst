macOS Launchpad/Apps.app
==============================

Did you loose your Launchpad launcher icon from your doc? Did you know its now called the Apps.app?

For how long I've been using macOS, I've got "Launchpad" stuck in my head. So, when you're looking for `Launchpad.app`` in your `Applications`` folder, make sure you're looking for the `Apps.app` instead.
