macOS Launchpad/Apps.app
==============================

Did you lose your Launchpad launcher icon from your dock? Did you know its now called the Apps.app?

I've used macOS for a long time, and I still think of it as "Launchpad". If you're looking for ``Launchpad.app`` in your ``/Applications`` folder, make sure you're looking for ``Apps.app`` instead.
